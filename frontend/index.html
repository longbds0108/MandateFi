What we
will not break

Six non-negotiables encoded directly into the contracts. Not conventions, not comments — Solidity rules the agent cannot bypass.

01

Agents never hold your keys

An agent is just an on-chain address with the right to propose. All sensitive actions require your signature from your own Sepolia wallet.

02

Withdrawals are always yours

You can withdraw your deposit at any time, including while the mandate is paused, revoked, or expired. Custody is independent of policy.

03

Per-tx and daily limits are hard caps

Every proposal is bounded by `perTxLimit` and `dailyLimit`. The daily window resets on-chain — not off-chain, not by the agent.

04

Approved destinations only

Funds cannot flow to an address outside your pre-approved set. Adding a destination is an on-chain transaction signed by you.

05

Pause stops thinking, not escape

Pause and revoke are instant. New proposals are denied immediately, but you keep every right to move your capital.

06

Every verdict is traceable

Each evaluation emits an event with the rule hit, the amount, the destination, and the timestamp. Nothing happens without a receipt.

Questions you
should be asking

IS MANDATEFI AUDITED OR PRODUCTION-READY?+
No. MandateFi is a testnet MVP running on Ethereum Sepolia. The yield oracle is owner-updated for demos and the agent runner is an off-chain script. Marked with // RIALO: in the source wherever a native primitive will replace a shortcut. Do not deposit real funds.
WHY ETHEREUM SEPOLIA AND NOT RIALO?+
Rialo does not yet have a public deployment environment. Sepolia lets us demonstrate the policy enforcement model today with a Circle-issued test USDC, while reserving the native-webcall and native-timer paths for a Rialo port once it is available.
CAN THE AGENT CHANGE MY MANDATE?+
No. Mandate mutation is restricted to the owner at the Solidity level with custom errors. The agent can only submit proposals; the registry is write-protected against the agent address by `onlyOwner` checks.
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A PROPOSAL IS DENIED?+
The call reverts with a typed custom error — e.g. ExceedsPerTxLimit(amount, limit) or DestinationNotApproved(address). The frontend decodes the selector and renders a plain-English reason in the policy trace. No funds move.
HOW DO I GET TEST USDC?+
Request from the Circle Sepolia faucet. The vault accepts Circle's issued USDC at 0x1c7D…7238. Gas is paid in Sepolia ETH, which you can obtain from standard faucets.
